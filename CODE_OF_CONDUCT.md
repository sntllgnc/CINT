# Code of conduct

Participation in Agent Floor must remain technically rigorous and professionally respectful.

Contributors must:

- critique claims, evidence, and code without attacking people;
- protect private repositories, credentials, traces, and identities;
- disclose uncertainty and distinguish reproduced facts from inference;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, and deliberate disruption;
- follow maintainers' decisions on scope, safety, and coordinated disclosure.

Report conduct or privacy concerns through the repository's private security-reporting channel. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when this standard is violated.
